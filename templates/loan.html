What is Loan EMI?

Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month. EMIs are used to pay off both interest and principal each month so that over a specified number of years, the loan is paid off in full.

EMI Formula

The standard mathematical formula to calculate EMI is:

E = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ / ((1 + r)ⁿ - 1)
  • E is EMI
  • P is Principal Loan Amount
  • r is rate of interest calculated on monthly basis (i.e., r = Rate of Annual interest/12/100)
  • n is loan term / tenure / duration in number of months

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How does the loan tenure affect my EMI?

A: A longer loan tenure reduces your monthly EMI amount, making it more affordable, but it significantly increases the total interest you will pay over the life of the loan.

Q: Can I reduce my EMI during the loan term?

A: Yes, you can reduce your EMI by making part-prepayments towards the principal amount, or by refinancing the loan at a lower interest rate.
